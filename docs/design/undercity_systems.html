Undercity

immersive sim · systems design v0.1

A runner in the drowned lower levels of Meridian. Every problem has three doors: force, stealth, or talking your way in.

This page is the systems reference for the first playable slice: the Sump Market hub, the sewer level and the junkyard level. It covers the character, inventory and equipment, dialog, disguise and perception, and how they feed each other.

01 · Design pillars

Five rules the levels are built around

Three doors

Each objective can be solved by force, stealth or social engineering, and usually by tech too. Every level has at least three routes to its key rooms.

Talk to anyone

Every hub NPC has a dialog tree. Hostiles become talkable when your disguise holds up to their scrutiny.

Smart enemies

Enemies differ in intelligence and senses. Bosses see through cheap disguises, and robot dogs smell you whatever you wear.

No dice

Checks are deterministic and shown up front: [Deception 3]. You plan a build instead of reloading a save.

It sticks

An alarm in the pump station costs a hostage. A dead boss means his gang stays hostile in the hub. State persists between levels.

02 · Character

Skill trees, XP and levels

Seven skills, ranks 0–5. Each rank unlocks a named perk and adds +1 to that skill's checks. Ranks 1–2 cost 1 point and ranks 3–5 cost 2. You start with 4 points and Deception 1. Each level up grants 2 more.

03 · Inventory & equipment

What you carry is what you can do

A Deus Ex-style grid: every item has a footprint, and ammo and consumables stack. Five equipment slots set armour, stealth and disguise. A ten-slot belt on keys 1–0 holds weapons, gadgets and consumables. Pressing the active weapon's key holsters it, and a holstered weapon keeps a disguise intact.

04 · Dialog

Trees with checks you can see

Dialog trees are data (data/dialog/*.json) validated at build time. A choice can have conditions (flags, items, credits, skills, disguise, quest state), one deterministic check, and effects (flags, items, credits, XP, quests, trade, turn hostile).

05 · Disguise & perception

Cover versus intelligence

Each clothing piece carries a faction tag. Disguise quality Q is the sum of the matching pieces worn: body 2, head 1, face 1. Cover = Deception + Q. An observer inside their scrutiny range (2 + 2 × I metres) accepts you only if Cover ≥ their intelligence I. Further away, a matching outfit passes at a glance.

07 · Hub

Sump Market

The bottom of Low Harbor: a rain-soaked market square under the maglev viaduct, with the lit windows of the megatowers filling the sky. Every NPC here can be talked to. The storm drain leads down to the sewers, and the MerSec gate opens onto Scrapyard Road.

08 · Level · sewer

The Drains: Rat Trap

Drain Rats territory under the Sump: brick collector tunnels, water channels and pump machinery, with a shanty camp in an overflow cistern. The Rats hold two sanitation workers in the pump station.

Force

Fight down the collector tunnel and through the camp. If Twitch hears the fight, he executes a hostage.

Stealth

Take the vent crawl from maintenance to the pump station. Knock Twitch out from behind, then pick the cage (Lockpicking 2) or use the pump room key.

Social

In a Rat jacket and respirator, pass the checkpoint with Skiv's password. Bluff Twitch ([Deception 2]), convince Mother Rat the city paid ([Deception 3] or [Persuasion 3]), or pay 500 credits.

Tech

Pick the flooded grate (Lockpicking 1) and hack the pump room door (Hacking 2). Mother's terminal holds her safe code.

Stakes

Both hostages alive earns the full fee and Petra's gratitude. A dead hostage turns Petra hostile and Silk pays less.

09 · Level · junkyard

Scrap King's Yard: Chop Job

A walled scrapyard at night: crushed-car stacks, container rows, a crane, a car crusher, searchlights and robot dogs. The target is a prototype nav core in the safe in Crusher's office. Bonus contracts: kill Crusher for Jax, and steal the Kings' ledger for Petra.

Front gate

Show Jax's gang pass, bluff the two guards in a Scrap Kings vest (I2), or bribe them. Inside, a vest lets you walk the chop shop floor.

Fence gap

Crouch through the west gap and past the dog patrol. Dogs smell through any disguise, so time it or use a noise maker or EMP.

Power & heights

Killing the generator blinds the searchlight and unlocks the gate, but it sends a foreman to check. Climb the container stack to the catwalk and drop through the skylight into the office.

Force

Four Kings in the chop shop, two at the gate, dogs and a boss with a cyber-arm. Loud and expensive.

The safe

Open it with Lockpicking 3, the combination from Crusher's terminal (Hacking 3), or by talking Crusher into opening it for a "buyer" ([Deception 4], full Kings outfit).
