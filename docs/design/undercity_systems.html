UndercityImmersive sim · Meridian, Low Harbor

A first-person immersive sim in the drowned bottom of a megacity. You are a runner who takes contracts from a fixer in the Sump Market, then gets into places the hard way, the quiet way or the talkative way. This page presents the design: three level maps, seven systems and the screens. The requirements live in openspec/changes/; each section names the change it presents.

F1

The slice

openspec/changes/undercity-architecture

The first slice is one hub and two missions. Everything in it serves one loop: take a contract in Low Harbor, choose how to get in, live with what happened. The build you choose (skills, gear, disguises) decides which doors you see.

Status what is real today

BuiltLow Harbor, the hub: built in Blender from its layout in eight sectors, with every NPC, door, container, terminal and exit placed. See docs/playtest/undercity_hub.md.
BuiltUndercity.Core: skills and XP, the pack and belt, prices, locks, disguises, the hub's law, dialog, quests, saves and the save rule, the NPC body table, with 173 tests. The Godot layer only wires it to scenes.
BuiltThe HUD, the wrist-deck (Inventory, Skills, Journal), the dialog and terminal screens, the title screen, the pause menu with saves, and options, from the approved mockups.
BuiltThe NPC bodies: 22 generated in Blender with MPFB2 from CC0 MakeHuman assets, sharing one CC0 animation library (UAL) and falling as Jolt ragdolls that come to rest. Adopted on the owner's conditional answer (H1 to H3); the look is judged in the hub.
BuiltWater, swimming and ways out (F14): the runner floats, swims, dives and can drown; 17 ladders and the ship's boarding ladders lead out, every point of water within 25 m of swimming of one. See docs/validation/2026-09-28-water-and-swimming.md.
BuiltThe Kestrel fires (F18): everyone can be shot by the one damage rule, people fight, flee, cower or surrender, gunfire turns MerSec, and death fades to the newest save. See docs/validation/2026-09-28-hub-combat.md.
Enforcedopenspec/specs/level-geometry: no z-fighting, frame reveals, every room carved, and every person clear of the level with their own collider. Every generator refuses a level that fails it, and the placement test checks the built hub.
ProposedThe Drains, the Yard, combat, the minigames and augmentations. Their sections below are still designs.

Dogs and disguises

A Kings outfit gets you past every human in the Yard, but the dogs smell you at 8 m through walls. Keep them docked (kennel, Hacking 2), EMP them for 20 s, lure them with a noise maker, or stay up high: dogs can't climb. Dutch warns you: "The dogs don't care what you're wearing."

Skills, perks, XP

openspec/changes/character-progression

Seven skills, five ranks each. Every rank is a perk, and every rank adds 1 to that skill's checks. Checks never roll: a check passes when rank + at most +1 from gear meets the DC. Ranks 1 and 2 cost 1 point and ranks 3 to 5 cost 2, so everything costs 56 points and level 20 gives 42. Try a build below.

Locks and devices

openspec/changes/world-interaction

One use key. The object answers with a prompt and a hold time from the same rule that decides the outcome, so the prompt can't lie. A lock tries its key, then a known code, then a lockpick, then a multitool. A tool is used up only when the lock opens.

    Water, swimming and ways out

    openspec/changes/water-and-swimming

    Owner, 2026-09-28: "Falling into the Bay there's no water physics, swimming, and no way to get back out." It was a flat, opaque surface with no collider: a fall sank you to the bed, 4.5 m under a quay you couldn't jump to. Now one set of rules serves every water body, the Drains' bypass included: wade, swim, hold your breath, tire slowly, and always have a way out within 25 m of swimming. The hub has 17 ladders: 12 on the Cut, one on the dry dock's quay and four over the side of the MV Anselm, which fills the dock. They are on the hub map (F2) as cyan ladders.

    Built (I1: first)Checked: the plan's exit rule, swim_test 18 of 18, ladders in the placement test

    The Cut in section measured heights; 60 px = 1 m

    Meridian's towers

    openspec/changes/hub-skyline

    Owner, 2026-09-28: "There should be mega skyscrapers surrounding our little slum area." Nothing in the hub stands above the 40 m foundation wall, and past the rooftops there is empty, fogged sky. The skyline puts the Halcyon Spire (440 m) and a ring of named megastructures around Low Harbor. They render unshaded, with lit windows and their own darker haze, so they read as towers, not fog ghosts.

    Proposed · decided (I7, I8, with searchlights) · fourth

    Plan layout metres; the hub is the lit rectangle; heights in metres

    Faces, skin and coloured gels

    openspec/changes/character-lighting

    Owner, 2026-09-28: faces are too dark in conversation, skin needs normal maps, and the runner needs a light source; coloured gels suit cyberpunk. Measured: Tank's face at the bar is 23 of 255 luma, while Lin under the shrine's lights is 161. Faces read only where the level happens to light them. The fix is new lights that touch only characters: the wrist-deck's glow, and a key and two gels when a conversation opens. It follows the owner's reference (Digital Camera's lighting guide: LOOP, KEY WITH A CLOSE SOFTBOX, COLOURED GELS).
