# UT99 reference set and style guide

The art direction for Brushfire is "Quake 2 / Unreal 1 era". Rather than trawling the whole
[Unreal Archive](https://unrealarchive.org/unreal-tournament/maps/index.html), this is a reference
set of roughly the top 100 UT99 maps (the official Epic maps plus the community classics that
come up again and again in "best maps" threads, the NaliCity hotlist and CliffyB's "Ownage"
picks) and a style guide distilled from them. The last section lists how each rule is applied
in our three levels.

Source keys: **T** ut99.org Top10 thread (t=163), **B** ut99.org "Best maps" (t=3220),
**C** ut99.org "best maps made by community" (t=1245), **O** ut99.org best outdoor maps
(t=13093), **N** NaliCity hotlist (**N★** featured map), **CB** CliffyB's "Ownage" picks,
**A** the 2001 duel rotation (arenafps.com), **X** press accolades, **W** Unreal Wiki map page.

## 2. Style guide

Items marked *(rule of thumb)* are our own inference rather than a quoted source.

### (a) Architectural vocabulary

- **Trim everywhere.** A separate, plain, contrasting trim texture at floor–wall joints, along ramp
  and walkway edges and on stair nosings (UnrealWiki). The stock sets (UTtech1/2, Skaarj, SkyCity)
  each ship matching door, trim, pillar and floor textures. *(rule of thumb)* 16–32 UU baseboard,
  a band at the ceiling, a middle band in rooms taller than 256 UU.
- **Pillars.** Square or 8-sided shafts with a wider base and top. Industrial: boxed girders with
  hazard-striped bases. Castle: chamfered stone. Egyptian: dense "halls of pillars" (Sesmar).
  Pillar pairs double as cover (Stalwart, Morbias, Gauntlet).
- **Doorways.** 128 UU tall. Thick frames with inset side posts and a lintel. Tech doors are
  hexagonal (Ratchet) or chamfered with sliding panels; castle doors are round or pointed arches
  (Agony).
- **Windows.** Few, small and heavily framed: tech slots, ornate castle windows, stained glass.
  Skylights and viewports onto the skybox double as landmarks (Distinctive, Asthenosphere).
- **Stairs and ramps.** 16 UU risers (24 max). Ramps are as common as stairs. Curved paired stairs
  for grand halls (Mazon).
- **Ceilings.** Nali: "masonry bearing wall / stacked stone / wood trusses and post-and-beam"
  (Unreal's level design doc). Industrial: girders, catwalk undersides, ducts. Hub rooms get domes
  (16-sided Liandri, octagonal Morbias, quarter-circle Grinder).
- **Floors.** Grates over slime or lava, block paving (Lian-X), metal panels (Metalmys),
  team-coloured brushed metal, all bordered with trim.
- **Pipes and grates.** Oversized overhead pipes (Synapse); pipes, vents and ducts wrapping the
  room (Closer); masked grates.
- **Bevels.** Low-poly 45° chamfers; 8–16-sided cylinders; curved corridors made of visible
  segments (Zeto, Command); lifts in wall slots (Agio); deliberate damage (cracked pipes, missing
  panes, stray blocks).

### (b) Theme palettes

- **Liandri industrial** – UTtech1–3, Metalmys, Mine, Lian-X, Factory, Indus1–5. Rust brown,
  gunmetal, hazard yellow/black, molten orange, teal or green slime.
- **Gothic castle** – ShaneChurch, UT, castle1, NaliCast, SkyCity, Crypt/UTcrypt. Grey and brown
  stone, warm torch orange, purple or blue moonlight, jewel-tone glass.
- **Nali / Egyptian temple** – Ancient, Egypt, EgyptPan, GenEarth/GenTerra, PhraelFx. Sandstone
  and ochre, turquoise water, hot sunlight.
- **Space / tech** – PlayrShp, Coret_FX, Starship, Skaarj, Queen, XbpFX/SpaceFX. Steel, cyan and
  blue light, black starfields, red alarm accents.

### (c) Lighting

- **Every light has a visible fixture** at the light's position, usually with a corona.
- **UnrealEd hue values:** 0 red, 40 yellow, 80 green, 160 blue, 200 violet. Saturation ≈ 64 is
  coloured, 255 is white.
- **Two complementary colours plus one accent:** warm fixtures, cool blue fill, red/green kept for
  gameplay cues (team sides, flashing red for hazards, spotlights to pull the player along).
- **Ambient and contrast:** zone ambient ≤ 32; shadows dark blue, never black; contrast without
  blown-out lights (CliffyB's "green out"); colour "without becoming gratuitous" and "let the
  architecture speak".
- **Effects:** flicker for broken fixtures, pulse for machinery, strobe for alarms. Lava and
  screens are unlit/self-lit. Volumetric lights for haze and light shafts.
- **Skies:** every exterior has a skybox – Earth (Face), purple night (Gothic), storm
  (Barricade), city skyline (Morpheus, Tempest).

### (d) Layout and gameplay

- **Scale.** 1 UU ≈ 1.9 cm (52.5 UU = 1 m, 16 UU = 1 ft). Player 78 × 34 UU. Ceilings 128 UU
  recommended (83 minimum). Halls ≥ 48 UU wide. Jumps ≈ 64–72 UU up, ≈ 344 UU across. Doors
  128 UU. *(rule of thumb)* corridors 128–256 UU wide, main rooms 512–1024 UU, atriums
  1024–2048 UU with tiers ≈ 256 UU apart. CliffyB: Turbine too tight, Deck16 too big, FWTBT just
  right.
- **Verticality.** Two or three tiers; ledges overlooking a central hub or courtyard.
- **Flow.** Avoid room–corridor–room. Loops, spirals, criss-crossing paths (Nitro, Pah, Chronos).
  A landmark in almost every room.
- **Movement.** Lifts and jump pads, each lift with its own sound; teleporters used sparingly.
- **Items.** Every weapon at least once, one of each armour type (Epic's rule). Power-ups where
  they are risky to reach.
- **Hazards.** Slime or lava pits, traps (Pressure, Fractal), edges you can fall to your death
  from.

### (e) Checklist: what makes a room read as UT99

1. Every floor–wall edge, ramp lip and stair nosing has a contrasting trim strip.
2. Every doorway has a thick framed jamb and a lintel, inset from the wall.
3. Pillars always have a base and a capital – never a bare prism.
4. Light fixtures are recessed or bracketed, with a corona, and cast a coloured pool of light.
5. One warm key colour, one cool fill colour, at most one saturated accent per room.
6. At least one opening onto a dramatic skybox, or a view down into a hazard.
7. A pit, pool or channel of slime, lava or water with a grate or bridge over it.
8. Oversized industrial props: giant pipes, fans, gauges, hazard stripes.
9. The ceiling is structural (beams, trusses, girders or a dome), never a flat plane.
10. At least two height levels, with a ledge or balcony overlooking the main floor.
11. Round spaces are built from visible 8/16/32-sided segments; corners chamfered at 45°.
12. 256² textures at ~1 texel per UU with detail textures on large surfaces.
13. Animated surfaces: scrolling screens, flowing lava, pulsing energy.
14. Deliberate damage: cracked panels, missing glass, rubble.
15. A unique landmark in each room: monolith, reactor, statue or skylight.

## 3. How Brushfire applies it

The levels are generated from scripts, so most rules are applied by code shared by all three
toolchains rather than by hand. The numbers below refer to the checklist in section 2(e).

| Rule | Where it lives | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Trim everywhere | `tools/godot/detailing.py` | Every room is an axis-aligned air box. For each wall, the openings cut by other rooms, doors and arches are subtracted, then baseboards, cornices and (tech rooms) a hazard band are emitted as boxes. The CSG level turns them into `CSGBox3D` brushes, the TrenchBroom map into detail brushes, and the Blender level into bevelled blocks. |
| 2 Framed doorways | `tools/blender/build_props.py`, `scenes/props/doorway.tscn` | A Blender doorway kit: thick jambs, pistons, a lintel with a status light bar, split sliding leaves, plus stone archway frames for open passages. |
| 3 Pillars have a base and a capital | `detailing.py`, level generators | Pilasters get a plinth, shaft and capital. Freestanding pillars (CSG exit pillars, TrenchBroom octagonal and hexagonal pillars, Blender columns) all have plinths and capitals. |
| 4 Visible fixtures with coronas | `gen_scenes.py` | Ceiling panels, caged wall lamps and door downlights. Each has an additive corona billboard and a baked light at the fixture. |
| 5 Warm key, cool fill, one accent | level generators | Warm fixtures, big cool-blue fill lights, plus green (exit) or orange (lava, furnace) as the accent. |
| Shadows dark blue, never black | `level_common.add_zone_ambient` | UT99's per-zone ambient, rebuilt for LightmapGI. Every room gets soft omni lights with a flat falloff in a low blue (#3b5aa8), baked like any other light. The lightmapper always traces shadows, so each fill stays inside its room like a zone. |
| 6 Opening onto a skybox | `shaders/sky_surface.gdshader`, `tools/fx/generate_skies.py` | Quake-style sky surfaces: a face with a `sky_*` material shows two scrolling, tileable layers projected on a squashed dome, plus an analytic moon or planet. Pump Station has a grated skylight onto a violet nebula with a planet. Slag Works has a smoke vent onto a furnace-lit storm. The Cistern has an oculus onto a moonlit night. A baked spotlight lines up with each moon. |
| 7 Hazard pit with a bridge | Slag Works | A lava channel with two grated bridges and hazard-striped curbs, plus a molten crucible. The Cistern has a sunken pit with railings. |
| 9 Structural ceiling | `detailing.ceiling_beams` | Girders span each flat-ceilinged room's short axis, lined up with the pilasters, so wall and ceiling read as one frame (corridors become portal frames). Their material contrasts with the ceiling. They skip light fixtures through `keep_out` boxes, and lights are placed in the bays between girders. The Cistern's vaults are already structural, so it has no girders. |
| 10 Two height levels | layouts | Pump Station has a catwalk and a raised platform. Slag Works has the north gallery and a jump pad. The Cistern has a sunken pit and a raised overflow gallery. |
| 11 Segmented round shapes | generators | 8- and 16-sided pumps, pillars and pipes, 32-segment vaults, and 9-voussoir arch rings. |
| 13 Animated surfaces | `scripts/World/SurfaceAnimator.cs` | Unreal-style panning textures. Slag Works' lava scrolls and its emission pulses. Sky layers scroll in the shader. |
| 15 A landmark per room | layouts | The pump hall's skylight and pumps, the foundry's lava channel and smoke vent, the crucible vat, the cistern's oculus, and the green exit pads. |

Scale: 1 m = 52.5 UU. The player is 1.8 m tall (UT's 78 UU is about 1.49 m; Brushfire uses a
Quake-style taller player). Doorways are 3.2 m (≈ 168 UU) and corridors 4 m (≈ 210 UU, within
the 128–256 UU corridor guideline). Main rooms are 12–32 m (630–1680 UU).
